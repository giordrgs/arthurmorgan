Arthur Morgan(infinite aura)



Arthur Morgan is the main protagonist of Red Dead 2. He is one of the most hot, complex and compelling characters of the game, or any game, for that matter.

From his detailed muscles and book-like story to one of the most and realistic textures and effects on a game character ever made, only losing to lucia from GTA 6, it is truly impressive what Rockstar accomplished in the game.

He also made a massive impact on the gaming and hentai industry with his realism, paired with impressive physics, such as slight input delay in actions to give the sense of a real human reacting to the user's input, beard that grows over time, his facial expressions when he's calm versus when he's shooting, him eating a huge ton of high-carbs food but still looking hot as fuck, and motion captured animations.
